# 0001: Five Levers of Polished UI

## What was learned

The student learned the five concrete, copy-paste-able levers that separate "default" UIs from polished ones: spacing (4px consistency scale), color (limited palette, no pure black), typography (Inter, size/weight/color hierarchy), borders/shadows (1px subtle, consistent radius), and animation (purposeful fade-up/scale/stagger). These are the minimum viable design concepts for directing AI coding tools effectively.

## Evidence

Student asked a specific comparison question referencing Brickify and cal.ai — concrete examples they could study. They self-assessed as "beginner" (can get basic layouts working but looks default-ish). The lesson was designed as a self-contained HTML file with before/after visual comparisons, a practice check, and an AI prompt template they can paste into vibe coding sessions.

## Implications

- The student now has vocabulary to ask for specific design improvements
- Next session should go deeper on one lever (color systems with Tailwind semantic tokens is highest leverage)
- The AI prompt template in the lesson gives them a repeatable workflow for future sessions
- The reference cheat sheet gives them a printable one-pager to keep at their desk
