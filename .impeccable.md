# BrickVal design context

## Design Context

### Users

LEGO collectors and sellers who want to identify and value a pile of figures quickly, often while sorting a collection. The key job is moving from a scattered physical set of items to a trustworthy market-value view with minimal friction.

### Brand Personality

Fast, playful, precise. The experience should create a short burst of discovery when users move from one-at-a-time scanning to bulk scanning, while still feeling like a polished native iOS utility rather than a game overlay.

### Aesthetic Direction

Energetic “power-up” motion layered onto BrickVal’s existing Robinhood-inspired portfolio hierarchy and active green value color. Motion should feel physical and directional: a boost into a larger scanning mode, followed by a calm, readable bulk workspace. Avoid persistent glow, noisy particles, bounce, and effects that compete with scan results.

### Design Principles

1. Make the mode change obvious: single scan becomes a wider, more capable workflow.
2. Use one signature boost moment, then get out of the way of the user’s results.
3. Keep the sound and motion synchronized but independently accessible through reduced-motion and mute behavior.
4. Preserve native iOS conventions, 44-point interaction targets, Dynamic Type, dark/light appearance, and VoiceOver clarity.
5. Keep the effect performant on small iPhones by favoring composited transforms and opacity over layout animation.
